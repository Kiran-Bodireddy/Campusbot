# B.B.A.,LL.B. Bachelor of Business Administration and Legum Baccalareus (Honours)

- **Degree Level**: Undergraduate (UG)
- **Awarded Credential**: B.B.A., LL.B (Hons.)
- **Faculty / College**: SRM School of Law
- **Department**: Department of Law
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 5 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 120
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 300,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
10, + 2 pass in regular/normal mode. Minimum 60% aggregate or equivalent letter grade/GPA in +2 board examination. Appeared in 2026 CLAT/SRMJEEL for 5 year integrated programs.

---

## Program Overview & Curriculum Scope
A 5-year integrated B.B.A., LL.B (Hons.) programme at SRM Institute of Science and Technology combining business administration fundamentals with legal education.

---

## Career Opportunities
Corporate Law, Business & Legal Advisory, Compliance, Contract Management
