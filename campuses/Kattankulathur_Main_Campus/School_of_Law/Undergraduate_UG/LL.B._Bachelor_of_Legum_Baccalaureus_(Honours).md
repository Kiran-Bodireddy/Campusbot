# LL.B. Bachelor of Legum Baccalaureus (Honours)

- **Degree Level**: Undergraduate (UG)
- **Awarded Credential**: LL.B (Hons.)
- **Faculty / College**: SRM School of Law
- **Department**: Department of Law
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 3 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 120
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 300,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
Any Degree with a minimum of 60% aggregate or equivalent letter grade/GPA. Appeared in 2026 SRMJEEL appeared for 3 year LLB program.

---

## Program Overview & Curriculum Scope
A 3-year LL.B (Hons.) programme at SRM Institute of Science and Technology covering core legal domains and professional law foundations.

---

## Career Opportunities
Legal Practice, Corporate Law, Litigation Support, Compliance
