## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
The eligibility condition is post graduate in Law from UGC approved university, with at least 55% of marks. A relaxation of 5% marks or an equivalent relaxation of grade may be allowed for those belonging to SC/ST/OBC (non-creamy layer/differently-abled). Stipend : All full-time PhD scholars who are selected for provisional registration during the admission year 2021 onwards will be paid Rs. 31,000/- per month for a maximum period of 3 years. Candidates who have qualified in UGC-NET (including JRF)/UGC-CSIR NET (including JRF)/GATE/GPAT will be paid Rs. 37,000/- per month for a maximum period of 3 years. The eligibility for availing a stipend every year, subsequent to the provisional registration, will be based on the progress made in research during the previous year, as part of the PhD.

---

## Program Overview & Curriculum Scope
The SRM Institute of Science and Technology Ph.D. in Law programme is designed to develop advanced legal research, analytical, and academic expertise in various areas of law. The course focuses on constitutional law, corporate law, cyber law, intellectual property rights, human rights, and interdisciplinary legal studies. Scholars work under experienced faculty guidance and contribute through research publications, seminars, and thesis work. The programme prepares candidates for careers in academia, legal consultancy, policy research, and judicial or corporate legal sectors.
