# BBA. Digital Marketing

- **Degree Level**: Undergraduate (UG)
- **Awarded Credential**: BBA
- **Faculty / College**: Faculty of Management
- **Department**: Department of Management
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 3 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 60
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 200,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
A Pass in Higher Secondary Examination with Minimum eligibility of aggregate of 50% in +2 (Preference will be given to students without any break in study).

---

## Program Overview & Curriculum Scope
A 3-year undergraduate BBA programme at SRM Institute of Science and Technology focused on modern marketing, digital platforms and brand growth fundamentals.

---

## Career Opportunities
Digital Marketing, Brand & Content, Performance Marketing, Marketing Analytics, Growth
