# M.B.A. Sports Management

- **Degree Level**: Postgraduate (PG)
- **Awarded Credential**: MBA
- **Faculty / College**: Faculty of Management
- **Department**: Department of Management
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 30
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 495,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
A Bachelor’s degree with minimum aggregate of 50%. Admission will be done through SRM Joint Entrance Examination for Management (SRMJEEM) followed by a Personal Interview. Students with eligible entrance scores in CAT/XAT/MAT/GMAT/NMAT/CMAT/TANCET will be granted direct consideration for the personal interview. Preference would be given to candidates with work experience.

---

## Program Overview & Curriculum Scope
MBA programme offered by SRM Institute of Science and Technology with focus on sports management.

---

## Career Opportunities
Sports Management, Sports Marketing, Event Management, Sports Operations, Sponsorship & Partnerships
