# B.Des. Interior Design

- **Degree Level**: Undergraduate (UG)
- **Awarded Credential**: B.DES
- **Faculty / College**: Faculty of Engineering & Technology
- **Department**: School of Architecture and Interior Design
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur(KTR)
- **Duration**: 4 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 30
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 150,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
A Pass in 10+2 (Higher Secondary) examination or any other equivalent examination of any authority recognized by SRMIST with a minimum of 60% aggregate/percentage of marks. The candidate can belong to any group of study (Science, Commerce, Fine Arts or any other stream). OR A Pass in Diploma in Civil/Architecture with a minimum of 60% aggregate/percentage of marks.

## Program Overview & Curriculum Scope
Comprehensive academic curriculum offered at SRM Institute of Science and Technology.
