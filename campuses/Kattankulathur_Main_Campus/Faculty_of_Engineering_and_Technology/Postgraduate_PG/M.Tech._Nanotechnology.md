# M.Tech. Nanotechnology

- **Degree Level**: Postgraduate (PG)
- **Awarded Credential**: M.TECH
- **Faculty / College**: Faculty of Engineering & Technology
- **Department**: Department of Physics and Nanotechnology
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 6
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 160,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
Eligibility for M.Tech Programs (Duration: 2 Years) Specialization Eligibility (A basic degree or equivalent in the following with a minimum aggregate of 50%) Nanotechnology B.E / B.Tech. (Any Specialization), M.Sc (Physics / Materials Science / Chemistry / Applied Chemistry / Bio Chemistry / Biotechnology) with mathematics as one of the subjects at B.Sc level (or) its equivalent.
