# M.Tech. Bio Technology

- **Degree Level**: Postgraduate (PG)
- **Awarded Credential**: M.TECH
- **Faculty / College**: Faculty of Engineering & Technology
- **Department**: Department of Biotechnology
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 30
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 160,000

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
Eligibility for M.Tech Programs (Duration: 2 Years) Specialization Eligibility (A basic degree or equivalent in the following with a minimum aggregate of 60%) Biotechnology B.E/B.Tech in Biotechnology/Biochemical Engineering/Chemical Engineering/Genetic Engineering/Bioinformatics/ Biomedical Engineering/Biomedical and Instrumentation Engineering/Bioprocess Engineering/Food Process Engineering/Integrated M.Sc Medical Microbiology/ Integrated M.Sc Biomedical Sciences/B.Pharm/B.Sc Agriculture/or M.Sc. (in any branch of Life Sciences/Biotechnology) (or) its equivalent.
