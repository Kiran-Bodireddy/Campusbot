# M.Tech. Genetic Engineering

- **Degree Level**: Postgraduate (PG)
- **Awarded Credential**: M.TECH
- **Faculty / College**: Faculty of Engineering & Technology
- **Department**: Department of Genetic Engineering
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 30
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 160,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
Eligibility for M.Tech. Programs (Duration: 2 Years) Specialization Eligibility (A basic degree or equivalent in the following with a minimum aggregate of 50%) Genetic Engineering B.E / B.Tech. in Genetic Engineering / Biotechnology / Industrial or Medical or General or Food Biotechnology / Biochemical Engineering / Bioinformatics / Bioprocess Engineering (or) B.Pharm (or) B.V.Sc. (or) B.F.Sc. (or) B.Sc. (Agri) / B.Sc. (Forestry) (or) MBBS (or) M.Sc. in any branch of Life Sciences (or) its equivalent.
