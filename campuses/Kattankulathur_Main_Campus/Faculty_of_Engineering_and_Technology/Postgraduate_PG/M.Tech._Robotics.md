# M.Tech. Robotics

- **Degree Level**: Postgraduate (PG)
- **Awarded Credential**: M.TECH
- **Faculty / College**: Faculty of Engineering & Technology
- **Department**: Department of Mechanical Engineering
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 2 Year
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 12
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 160,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
Eligibility for M.Tech Programs (Duration: 2 Years) Specialization Eligibility (A basic degree or equivalent in the following with a minimum aggregate of 50%) Robotics B.E / B.Tech. in Mechanical Engg. / Production Engg. / Industrial Engg. / Automobile Engg. / Aerospace / Aeronautical Engg. / Mechatronics Engg. / ECE / EEE / ICE / CSE (or) AMIE (Mech) / AMAeSI(Aero) / IMechE (or) any equivalent degree in the above Disciplines
