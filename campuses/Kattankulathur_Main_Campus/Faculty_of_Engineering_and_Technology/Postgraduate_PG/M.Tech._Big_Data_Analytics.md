# M.Tech. Big Data Analytics

- **Degree Level**: Postgraduate (PG)
- **Awarded Credential**: M.TECH
- **Faculty / College**: Faculty of Engineering & Technology
- **Department**: Department of Data Science And Business Systems
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 10
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 160,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
Engineering Background: BE/BTech any Stream with 50% marks. AMIE is accepted. MBA/M. Com/MA are not eligible Evening College & Distance education degrees are not accepted. All the degrees obtained must be through Full-Time mode. Degrees from UGC approved universities only will be accepted.
