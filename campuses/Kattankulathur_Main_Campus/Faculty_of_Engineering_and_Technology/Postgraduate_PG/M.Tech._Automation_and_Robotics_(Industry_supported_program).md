# M.Tech. Automation and Robotics (Industry supported program)

- **Degree Level**: Postgraduate (PG)
- **Awarded Credential**: M.TECH
- **Faculty / College**: Faculty of Engineering & Technology
- **Department**: Department of Electronics & Instrumentation Engineering
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 12
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 160,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
Four year Bachelor’s Degree in Engineering (except B.Tech. / B.Tech. w/s in Biotechnology, Food Technology), M.Sc (IT / Electronics) or its equivalent with a minimum aggregate of 50%

---

## Program Overview & Curriculum Scope
Comprehensive academic curriculum offered at SRM Institute of Science and Technology.
