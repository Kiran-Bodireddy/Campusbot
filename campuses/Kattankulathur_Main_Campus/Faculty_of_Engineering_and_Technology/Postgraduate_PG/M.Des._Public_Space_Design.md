# M.Des. Public Space Design

- **Degree Level**: Postgraduate (PG)
- **Awarded Credential**: M.DES
- **Faculty / College**: Faculty of Engineering & Technology
- **Department**: School of Architecture and Interior Design
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur(KTR)
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 12
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 175,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
50% aggregate in Bachelor of Design or Bachelor of Architecture degree course or any other equivalent qualification having a minimum duration of 4 years recognized by SRMIST/Competent Authority. Criteria 1: CEED Score + (Personal Interview) at SRMIST Criteria 2: Personal Interview at SRMIST

## Program Overview & Curriculum Scope
Comprehensive academic curriculum offered at SRM Institute of Science and Technology.
