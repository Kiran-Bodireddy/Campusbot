# M.Arch. Architectural Design

- **Degree Level**: Postgraduate (PG)
- **Awarded Credential**: M.ARCH
- **Faculty / College**: Faculty of Engineering & Technology
- **Department**: School of Architecture and Interior Design
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 20
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 100,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
50% aggregate in Bachelor of Architecture degree course or any other equivalent qualification recognized by the council of Architecture/Competent Authority. Personal interview will be conducted by the School Of Architecture and Interior Design, SRMIST

---

## Program Overview & Curriculum Scope
Comprehensive academic curriculum offered at SRM Institute of Science and Technology.
