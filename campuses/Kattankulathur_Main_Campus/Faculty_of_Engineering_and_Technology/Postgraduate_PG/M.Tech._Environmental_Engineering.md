# M.Tech. Environmental Engineering

- **Degree Level**: Postgraduate (PG)
- **Awarded Credential**: M.TECH
- **Faculty / College**: Faculty of Engineering & Technology
- **Department**: Department of Civil Engineering
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 12
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 160,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
Eligibility for M.Tech. Programs (Duration: 2 Years) Specialization Eligibility (A basic degree or equivalent in the following with a minimum aggregate of 50%) Environmental Engineering B.E. / B.Tech. (Civil / Civil Engineering with Computer Applications / Civil Infrastructure / Architectural Engg. / Chemical / Biotechnology), or M.Sc. (Environmental Science / Ecology / Environmental Ecology / Environmental Chemistry) (or) its equivalent.
