- **Awarded Credential**: M.TECH

- **Department**: Department of Mechanical Engineering
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 12
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 160,000

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
Eligibility for M.Tech Programs (Duration: 2 Years) Specialization Eligibility (A basic degree or equivalent in the following with a minimum aggregate of 50%) Computer Aided Design B.E / B.Tech. in Mechanical Engg. / Production Engg. / Automobile Engg. / Aerospace / Aeronautical Engg (or) AMIE (Mech) / AMAeSI(Aero) / IMechE. (or) any equivalent degree in the above Disciplines.
