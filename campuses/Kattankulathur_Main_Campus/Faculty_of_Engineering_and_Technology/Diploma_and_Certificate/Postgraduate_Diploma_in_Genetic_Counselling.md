## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
MSc/BE/BTech in any branch of life sciences, or an undergraduate degree in medical, dental, alternative medicine, or allied health sciences, or an equivalent qualification, with a minimum aggregate of 50% marks in the qualifying examination. Candidates with a higher degree in these disciplines are also eligible. Preference will be given to candidates with a background in genetics or clinical sciences and/or work experience in hospitals or support groups for genetic diseases.

---

## Program Overview & Curriculum Scope
The Postgraduate Diploma in Genetic Counselling at SRM Institute of Science and Technology is designed to equip students with the knowledge and practical skills required to guide individuals and families through genetic testing, diagnosis, and inherited health conditions. The programme integrates concepts of human genetics, genomics, counselling techniques, ethics, and clinical applications, preparing graduates to support informed healthcare decisions and contribute effectively to the growing field of precision medicine and genetic healthcare.
