---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
1. Candidates who have passed Medical Post-graduate clinical courses and Super Specialty degree courses recognized by the MCI/NMC.2. Candidates should have passed MD (OG)/ MS (OG) and MCh and DNB, FRCS, MRCP, MRCS, FRACS and AB of OG.3. Candidates who have studied in foreign countries (Outside India) and passed in Clinical Courses in OG, the equivalent certificate issued by the MCI/NMC.4. Completed the age of 25 years.5. Minimum aggregate of 50% marks from a recognized University in India.
