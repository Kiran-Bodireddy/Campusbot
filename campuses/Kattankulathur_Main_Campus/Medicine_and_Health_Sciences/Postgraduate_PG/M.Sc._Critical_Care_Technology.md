# M.Sc. Critical Care Technology

- **Degree Level**: Postgraduate (PG)
- **Awarded Credential**: M.Sc
- **Faculty / College**: Medicine & Health Sciences
- **Department**: Allied Health Sciences
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 20
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 125,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
1. Age limit: Completed the age of 21 years on or before 31st December of the year of admission to the program.2. Under-graduate degree from a recognized University with minimum 50% marks or CGPA of 5.0.3. In case of students from foreign Universities, they should have qualifications recognised as equivalent to an Under-graduate degree.4. Qualifying degree: B.Sc. Critical Care Technology/ B.Sc. Operation Theatre and Anaesthesia Technology/ B.Sc. Anaesthesia Technology/ B.Sc. Emergency and Trauma Care Technology/ B.Sc. Cardiac Care Technology/ B.Sc. Perfusion Technology/ B.Sc. Respiratory Care Technology/ B.Sc. Physician Assistant/B.Sc. Renal Dialysis Technology.

Postgraduate M.Sc programme offered by SRM Institute of Science and Technology in Critical Care Technology.
