# MPH – Applied Health Research

- **Degree Level**: Postgraduate (PG)
- **Awarded Credential**: MPH
- **Faculty / College**: Medicine & Health Sciences
- **Department**: SRM School of Public Health
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 10
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 300,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
1. Master of Public Health – Applied Health Research (AHR) is open to graduates from all academic streams with a minimum of 50% marks. 2. An understanding of basic statistics and proficiency in computer application is desired.
