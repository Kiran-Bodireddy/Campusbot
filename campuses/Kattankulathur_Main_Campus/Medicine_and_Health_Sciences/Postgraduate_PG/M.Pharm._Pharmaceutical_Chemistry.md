# M.Pharm. Pharmaceutical Chemistry

- **Degree Level**: Postgraduate (PG)
- **Awarded Credential**: M.PHARM
- **Faculty / College**: Medicine & Health Sciences
- **Department**: SRM College of Pharmacy
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 10
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 125,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
1. Candidate shall have passed a Bachelor’s degree in Pharmacy from a PCI approved institution.2. Minimum of 50% aggregate of marks in Bachelor’s Degree.3. Students should submit Pharmacist Registration Certificate obtained from the respective state pharmacy council at the time admission.4. Age: Minimum age limit is 21 years on or before 31st December of year of Admission.
