# M.Sc. Medical Microbiology

- **Degree Level**: Postgraduate (PG)
- **Awarded Credential**: M.SC
- **Faculty / College**: Medicine & Health Sciences
- **Department**: Allied Health Sciences
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 3 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 10
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 200,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
1. Completed the age of 21 years on or before 31st December of the year of admission to the program.2. Undergraduate degree from a recognized University with minimum 50% marks or CGPA of 5.0.3. In case of students from foreign Universities, they should have qualifications recognized as equivalent to an Undergraduate degree.4. Academic Qualification: B.Sc., (Zoology, Microbiology, Chemistry, Medical Laboratory Technology, Biotechnology, Life Sciences) / BDS.
