# M.Sc. Nurse Practitioner in Critical Care (NPCC)

- **Degree Level**: Postgraduate (PG)
- **Awarded Credential**: M.SC
- **Faculty / College**: Medicine & Health Sciences
- **Department**: SRM College of Nursing
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 5
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 150,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
1. B.Sc. Nursing/B Sc Hons. Nursing/Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing 2. Percentage of marks: Minimum 55 %aggregate of marks in the qualifying examination 3. Years of Experience: Minimum one year of clinical experience after B.Sc Nursing or Minimum one year of work experience prior or after Post Basic B.Sc Nursing, preferably in any critical care setting before enrollment. 4. The candidate should be a registered Nurse and registered Midwife or equivalent with any State Nursing Registration Council.
