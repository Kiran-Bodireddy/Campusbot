# M.Sc. Urology Technology

- **Degree Level**: Postgraduate (PG)
- **Awarded Credential**: M.SC
- **Faculty / College**: Medicine & Health Sciences
- **Department**: Allied Health Sciences
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 5
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 100,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
1. Age limit: Completed the age of 21 years on or before 31st December of the year of admission to the program.2. Under-graduate degree from a recognized University with minimum 50% marks or CGPA of 5.0.3. In case of students from foreign Universities, they should have qualifications recognised as equivalent to an Under-graduate degree.4. Qualifying degree: B.Sc. in Urology Technology/ B.Sc. Nursing/B.Sc. Physician Assistant.
