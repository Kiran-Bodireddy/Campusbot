- **Awarded Credential**: M.SC
- **Faculty / College**: Medicine & Health Sciences
- **Department**: SRM College of Nursing
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 5
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 90,000

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
1. B.Sc. Nursing/B Sc Hons. Nursing/Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing Percentage of marks: Minimum 55 %aggregate of marks in qualifying examination.2. Years of Experience: Minimum one year of work experience after B.Sc Nursing or Minimum one year of work experience prior or after Post Basic B.Sc Nursing.
