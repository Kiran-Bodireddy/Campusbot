## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
Candidates who have completed 12th grade in any stream/ group with a minimum of 50% of marks in aggregate are eligible for admission to MPH (Integrated) program. Candidates who have studied Sciences, Mathematics, Humanities and Social Sciences, Business and commerce-based electives, Visual; Performing and Creative Arts, Health and Physical Education, and General Studies can apply for admission. International Candidates must have completed 12 years of formal education equivalent to the India’s 10+2 system. Career Path On completion of the BSc- MPH (Integrated) five-year program, the students begin their career as program officer, project officer or research officer in a national or international NGO, CSR initiatives, government health department or research organization. Progressing in their careers, they ascend to state-level managers, eventually reaching national-level management positions. Some specialize in specific areas like Monitoring, Learning and Evaluation, Research, Community Mobilization, Advocacy, Policy, TB, HIV/AIDS, Family Planning, Gender Equity, Environmental Health, Health Communication, Health Economics, etc. Students can also pursue a Ph.D. and get into academia.
