# M.Sc. Biostatistics and Epidemiology

- **Degree Level**: Postgraduate (PG)
- **Awarded Credential**: M.SC
- **Faculty / College**: Medicine & Health Sciences
- **Department**: SRM School of Public Health
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 10
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 225,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
1. The MSc- Biostatistics and Epidemiology program is open to graduates from all streams with a minimum of 50% marks. 2. They must have the knowledge of basic mathematics. Candidates with undergraduation in Statistics, Data Sciences, Computer Sciences, or Commerce are encouraged to apply.
