# M.Sc. Medical Anatomy

- **Degree Level**: Postgraduate (PG)
- **Awarded Credential**: M.SC
- **Faculty / College**: Medicine & Health Sciences
- **Department**: Allied Health Sciences
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 3  Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 5
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 100,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
1. Completed the age of 21 years on or before 31st December of the year of admission to the program.2. Undergraduate degree from a recognized University with minimum 50% marks or CGPA of 5.0.3. In case of students from foreign Universities, they should have qualifications recognized as equivalent to an Undergraduate degree.4. Academic qualification: B.Sc. degree course in Zoology as the main subject with Botany as ancillary subject and B.Sc. Botany/ B.Sc. degree in Life Sciences, Bio-Science or Biology/ Professional course such as B.S.M.S., B.U.M.S., B.A.M.S., B.H.M.S., BDS., BPT., BOT., MPT & MOT/ B.Sc in Anatomy
