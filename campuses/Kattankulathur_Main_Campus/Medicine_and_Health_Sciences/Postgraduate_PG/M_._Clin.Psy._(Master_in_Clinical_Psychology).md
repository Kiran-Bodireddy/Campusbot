## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
Minimum educational requirment for admission to this course will be 4 years B.A./B.Sc., degree in Psychology from a university recognized by the UGC with a minimum of 55% marks in aggregate. For SC/ST category, minimum of 50% marks in aggregate is essential, as per GOI. The candidates who have completed their graduation program in 3 years of Psychology shall have to pass fourth year Bridge Course or first year of Masters Programme in Psychology with minimum of 55% of marks are also eligible. Placement Opportunities They can work as an Independent Clinical Psychologist with Valid RCI CRR license inHospitals, Mental Health Institutions, NGOs, Rehabilitation Centres, EducationalInstitutions, Research Projects, Government Institutes and as policy makers. Higher Studies Opportunities Ph.D/Psy.D in Clinical Psychology/Fellowship/Postdoctoral Fellowship Selection Through A selection committee that includes Head of theDepartment of Clinical Psychology shall make admission on the basisof an entrance examination, consisting of a written test and interview.The weightage of entrance test shall be 70% whereas the interview will be of 30%.
