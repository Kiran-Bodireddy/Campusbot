# M.O.T- Musculoskeletal Sciences

- **Degree Level**: Postgraduate (PG)
- **Awarded Credential**: Master's
- **Faculty / College**: Medicine & Health Sciences
- **Department**: SRM College of Occupational Therapy
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 3
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 210,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
1.Candidates must have passed Bachelor of Occupational Therapy (B.OT)on regular mode from recognised university. 2.Admission on the basis of entrance exams by respective University. 3.Post Graduate entrance criteria requires to be compulsory registered member of National Commission for Allied and Healthcare Professions (NCAHP)

---

## Program Overview & Curriculum Scope
Master's programme offered by SRM Institute of Science and Technology in Occupational Therapy with Orthopaedics focus.

---

## Career Opportunities
Occupational Therapy, Rehabilitation, Orthopaedics, Clinical Practice
