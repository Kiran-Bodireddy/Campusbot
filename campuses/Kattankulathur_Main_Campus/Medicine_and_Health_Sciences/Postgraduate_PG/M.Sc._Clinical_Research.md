# M.Sc. Clinical Research

- **Degree Level**: Postgraduate (PG)
- **Awarded Credential**: M.SC
- **Faculty / College**: Medicine & Health Sciences
- **Department**: Allied Health Sciences
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 15
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 125,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
1. Age limit: Completed the age of 21 years on or before 31st December of the year of admission to the program.2. Under-graduate degree from a recognized University with minimum 50% marks or CGPA of 5.0.3. In case of students from foreign Universities, they should have qualifications recognised as equivalent to an Under-graduate degree.4. Qualifying degree: B.Sc Biochemistry/ B.Sc Microbiology/ B.Tech Biotechnology/ B.Pharm/B.Sc. Nursing/B.Sc. Statistics/ MBBS/ BDS/ Any B.Sc Allied Health Sciences/B.Sc. Biotechnology/ B.Sc. Medical Biotechnology/ B.Tech Biomedical Engineering/ B.Sc. Biomedical Science/ B.Sc. Clinical Research/ B.Sc. Bioinformatics/ Health Informatics/ Data Science.
