# M.Sc. Medical Surgical Nursing

- **Degree Level**: Postgraduate (PG)
- **Awarded Credential**: M.Sc
- **Faculty / College**: Medicine & Health Sciences
- **Department**: SRM College of Nursing
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 5
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 90,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
1. B.Sc. Nursing/B Sc Hons. Nursing/Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing 2. Percentage of marks: Minimum 55 %aggregate of marks in the qualifying examination 3. Years of Experience: Minimum one year of work experience after B.Sc Nursing or Minimum one year of work experience prior or after Post Basic B.Sc Nursing. 4. The candidate should be a regisered Nurse and registered Midwife or equivalent with any State Nursing Registration Council.

---

## Program Overview & Curriculum Scope
Postgraduate nursing programme offered by SRM Institute of Science and Technology in Medical Surgical Nursing.

---

## Career Opportunities
Medical Surgical Nursing, Clinical Nursing, Hospital Care, Advanced Nursing Practice
