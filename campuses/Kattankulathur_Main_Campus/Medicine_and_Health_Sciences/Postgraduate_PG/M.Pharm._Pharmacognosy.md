# M.Pharm. Pharmacognosy

- **Degree Level**: Postgraduate (PG)
- **Awarded Credential**: M.PHARM
- **Faculty / College**: Medicine & Health Sciences
- **Department**: SRM College of Pharmacy
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 10
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 125,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
1. The candidate shall have passed a Bachelor’s degree in Pharmacy from a PCI-approved institution.2. A minimum of 55% aggregate marks in the Bachelor’s degree is required.3. Students must submit a Pharmacist Registration Certificate obtained from the respective State Pharmacy Council at the time of admission.4. Age: The minimum age limit is 21 years on or before 31st December of the year of admission.
