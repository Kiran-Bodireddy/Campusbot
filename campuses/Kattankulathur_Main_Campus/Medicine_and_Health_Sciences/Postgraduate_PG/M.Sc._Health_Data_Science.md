# M.Sc. Health Data Science

- **Degree Level**: Postgraduate (PG)
- **Awarded Credential**: M.SC
- **Faculty / College**: Medicine & Health Sciences
- **Department**: SRM School of Public Health
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 15
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 260,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
1. MSc- Health Data Science is open to graduates from all streams with a minimum of 50% marks. However, the candidate must have earned a certificate in computer programming such as C programming, Python, SQL, etc. from a recognized institution or from Swayam NPTL. 2. Candidates with undergraduation in Engineering, Computer Sciences, Biotechnology, Health Informatics, Pharmacy etc. are encouraged to apply.
