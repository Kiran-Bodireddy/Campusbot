## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
Students who have completed 12th class with science stream of equivalent will be eligible to apply for this programme. Placement Opportunities The candidates will get licensure under RCI as “Clinical Assistant (Clinical Psychology).These professionals will primarily work as frontline mental health workers.They can work under the Supervision of Master’s-level trained Clinical Psychologistsin Hospitals, Mental Health Institutions, Disability related Institutions and NGOs. Higher Studies Opportunities The candidates can pursue higher education in Clinical Psychology, and they can have the option to pursue masters in other branches of psychology as well in mental health. Selection Through The selection will be based on their performance in the Entrance Test and selection will determined by the basis of marks obtained in entrance test conducted by the respective University/Affiliating University/ Institution.
