# B.Sc. Nursing

- **Degree Level**: Undergraduate (UG)
- **Awarded Credential**: B.Sc Nursing
- **Faculty / College**: Medicine & Health Sciences
- **Department**: SRM College of Nursing
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 4  Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 100
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 265,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
1. Candidates must have passed the 12th Standard (10+2) examination with Science subjects, securing a minimum of 60% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (PCB). A pass in English is also required.2. The minimum age for admission is 17 years and the maximum age is 35 years, as of 31st December of the year of admission.

---

## Program Overview & Curriculum Scope
A 4-year undergraduate nursing programme at SRM Institute of Science and Technology focused on clinical foundations, patient care skills and professional nursing practice.

---

## Career Opportunities
Nursing Practice, Clinical Care, Hospital & Community Health
