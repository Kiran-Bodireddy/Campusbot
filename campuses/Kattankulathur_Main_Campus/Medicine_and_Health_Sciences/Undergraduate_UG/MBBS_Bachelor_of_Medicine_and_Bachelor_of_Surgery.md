# MBBS Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery

- **Degree Level**: Undergraduate (UG)
- **Awarded Credential**: MBBS
- **Faculty / College**: Medicine & Health Sciences
- **Department**: SRM Medical Research Centre
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 4.5 Years + 1 Year Internship
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 250  (Including 3 NRI)
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: Kindly refer to NEET MBBS Admissions 2026-2027 Information Bulletin
