# P.B.B.Sc. Nursing

- **Degree Level**: Undergraduate (UG)
- **Awarded Credential**: B.SC
- **Faculty / College**: Medicine & Health Sciences
- **Department**: SRM College of Nursing
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 30
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 60,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
1. Candidate must have passed Diploma in General Nursing and Midwifery (GNM) from an institution recognised by the Indian Nursing Council. 2. Possess a minimum of one year experience as a staff nurse 3. Candidate must be a registered Nurse and Midwife with the Tamil Nadu Nursing & Midwives Council or any other State Nursing Council.
