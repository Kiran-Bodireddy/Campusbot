# Post Basic Diploma Critical Care Nursing

- **Degree Level**: Diploma / Certificate
- **Awarded Credential**: N/A
- **Faculty / College**: Medicine & Health Sciences
- **Department**: SRM College of Nursing
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 1 Year
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 5
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 35,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
1. Candidates must have completed B.Sc. Nursing (Regular) Degree, Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing (Regular) Degree, or General Nursing and Midwifery (GNM) course with a minimum of 50% marks, recognized by the Indian Nursing Council, and registered with the State Nursing Council. 2. Minimum one-year clinical experience after B.Sc Nursing as a staff nurse preferably in the critical care unit
