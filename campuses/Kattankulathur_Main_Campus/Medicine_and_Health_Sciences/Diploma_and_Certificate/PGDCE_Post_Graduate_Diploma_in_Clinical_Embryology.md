# PGDCE Post Graduate Diploma in Clinical Embryology

- **Degree Level**: Diploma / Certificate
- **Awarded Credential**: PGDCE
- **Faculty / College**: Medicine & Health Sciences
- **Department**: Allied Health Sciences
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 1 Year
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 2
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 300,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
1. MBBS degree recognized by the MCI/ NMC/ Life Sciences Post Graduation (M.Sc) Candidates.2. Candidates who have studied in foreign countries (outside India) and passed in clinical courses, the equivalent certificate issued by MCI/NMC.3. Completed the age of 25 years.4. Minimum aggregate of 50% marks from a recognized University in India

Comprehensive academic curriculum offered at SRM Institute of Science and Technology.
