# Pharm.D – Doctor of Pharmacy (Post Baccalaureate)

- **Degree Level**: Doctoral / Ph.D.
- **Awarded Credential**: PHARM.D
- **Faculty / College**: Medicine & Health Sciences
- **Department**: SRM College of Pharmacy
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 3 Years (Including Internship)
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 10
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 250,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
Candidates shall have passed a Bachelor’s degree in Pharmacy from a PCI approved institution. Minimum of 50% aggregate of marks in Bachelor’s Degree. Students should submit Pharmacist Registration Certificate obtained from the respective State Pharmacy Council at the time admission. Age: Minimum age limit is 21 years on or before 31st December of year of Admission.
