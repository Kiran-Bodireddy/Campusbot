## Program Overview & Curriculum Scope
The School of Public Health offers a full-time Ph.D. program. Candidates are selected on the basis of a written exam and the presentation of their research proposal. Upon selection and enrollment, candidates complete two mandatory core courses focused on research methods and research and publication ethics. Subsequently, they engage in two elective courses aligned with their chosen topic of research. Following a comprehensive viva examination, candidates finalize the research topic. Studying the selected topic thoroughly, they undertake research to answer the identified research questions. Each candidate is required to publish a minimum of two research articles annually in Scopus/ wos journals and to present their work at conferences. Based on successful defense of the research findings before a doctoral committee, the candidate is conferred with a Ph.D. Subject to available vacancies, entrance examinations for admission to Ph.D. are conducted twice a year. Full-time Ph.D. scholars may qualify for institutional fellowship support of Rs. 25,000 per month. The minimum duration for the program is three years.
