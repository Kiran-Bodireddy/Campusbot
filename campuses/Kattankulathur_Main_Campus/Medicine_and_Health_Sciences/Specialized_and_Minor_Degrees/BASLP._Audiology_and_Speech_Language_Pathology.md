## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
1.Canditates seeking admission to the B.ASLP program should have passed 10+2 examination or an equivalent examination conducted by the Pre-University Board of Education or an equivalent Board of State or Central Government securing a minimum of 50% marks. 2.The applicants/canditates should have studied any two or more subjects in the Science Stream in the qualifying examination. 3. The eligibility of persons with disability shall be assessed by a committee consisting of an expert in Speech Language Pathology, Audiology, Clinical Psychology, Paediatrics and Otolaryngology, among others. 4. In general, admission of students under “Disability Category” for the B.ASLP program shall be governed by the same guidelines (No.U.14021-8-2023-UGMEB/dated the 12th June 2023) issued by the National Medical Commission for admission of students under “Disability Category” to the MBBS program. 5. Foreign applicants shall obtain equivalency certificate from the Association of Indian Universities.
