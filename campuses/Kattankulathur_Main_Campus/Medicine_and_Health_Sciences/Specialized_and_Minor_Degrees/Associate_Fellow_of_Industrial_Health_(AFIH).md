# Associate Fellow of Industrial Health (AFIH)

- **Degree Level**: Specialized / Other
- **Awarded Credential**: N/A
- **Faculty / College**: Medicine & Health Sciences
- **Department**: SRM School of Public Health
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 3 Months
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 25
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 100,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
MBBS Degree from an Institution recognized by the Medical Council of India, Completion of Internship, Permanent Registration with the State Medical Council/Medical Council of India. Minimum one year experience in Industry/Occupational health related work or 2 years in general practice after completion of internship.

---

## Program Overview & Curriculum Scope
Comprehensive academic curriculum offered at SRM Institute of Science and Technology.
