# B.Com. Information System and Management

- **Degree Level**: Undergraduate (UG)
- **Awarded Credential**: B.Com
- **Faculty / College**: Faculty of Liberal Arts & Business Studies
- **Department**: Department of Commerce
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 3 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 30
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 130,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
A pass in 10 + 2 (Higher Secondary) any group with a minimum aggregate of 50%.

---

## Program Overview & Curriculum Scope
Undergraduate B.Com programme offered by SRM Institute of Science and Technology in Information System and Management.

---

## Career Opportunities
Business Operations, Accounting & Finance, Business Information Systems, Management Support
