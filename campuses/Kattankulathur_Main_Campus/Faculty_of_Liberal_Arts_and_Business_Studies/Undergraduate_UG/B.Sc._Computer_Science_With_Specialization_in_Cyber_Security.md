# B.Sc. Computer Science With Specialization in Cyber Security

- **Degree Level**: Undergraduate (UG)
- **Awarded Credential**: B.SC
- **Faculty / College**: Faculty of Liberal Arts & Business Studies
- **Department**: Department of Computer Science
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 3 years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 120
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 130,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
A pass in 10 + 2 (Higher Secondary) with a minimum aggregate of 50% (or)A pass in Diploma in Engineering (10+3 or 10+2+2 Pattern) from a recognized government or competent authority with a minimum aggregate of 50%. Mandatory subjects :Atleast one of Mathematics / Business Mathematics / Statistics / Allied Mathematics.
