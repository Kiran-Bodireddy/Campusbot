- **Duration**: 3 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 180
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 150,000

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
A pass in 10 + 2 (Higher Secondary) with a minimum aggregate of 50% (or) A pass in Diploma in Engineering (10+3 or 10+2+2 Pattern) from a recognized government or competent authority with a minimum aggregate of 50%. Mandatory subjects :10 + 2 (Higher Secondary) with Mathematics / Business Mathematics / Statistics / Business Statistics/ Allied Mathematics / Computer Science/ Computer Application as one of the core subjects.
