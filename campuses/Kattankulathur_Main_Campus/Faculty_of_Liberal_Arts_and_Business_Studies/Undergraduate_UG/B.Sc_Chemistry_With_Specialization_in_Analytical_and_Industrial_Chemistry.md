# B.Sc – Chemistry With Specialization in Analytical and Industrial Chemistry

- **Degree Level**: Undergraduate (UG)
- **Awarded Credential**: B.Sc
- **Faculty / College**: Faculty of Liberal Arts & Business Studies
- **Department**: Department of Chemistry
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 3 Years*
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 15
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 75,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
A pass in 10 + 2 (Higher Secondary) with a minimum aggregate of 50%Mandatory subjects :1. Physics2. Chemistry3. Mathematics or Biology

---

## Program Overview & Curriculum Scope
Undergraduate B.Sc Chemistry programme offered by SRM Institute of Science and Technology with specialisation in Analytical and Industrial Chemistry.

---

## Career Opportunities
Analytical Chemistry, Industrial Chemistry, Laboratory Operations, Quality Control
