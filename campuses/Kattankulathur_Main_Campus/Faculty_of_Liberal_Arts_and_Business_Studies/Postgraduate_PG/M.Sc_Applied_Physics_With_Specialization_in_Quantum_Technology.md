# M.Sc – Applied Physics With Specialization in Quantum Technology

- **Degree Level**: Postgraduate (PG)
- **Awarded Credential**: M.SC
- **Faculty / College**: Faculty of Liberal Arts & Business Studies
- **Department**: Department of Physics
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 15
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 75,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
1. A pass in B.Sc. Physics (or) B.Sc. (Hons) Physics (or) B.S. Physics (or) B.Sc. Triple Major with Physics as one of the major subjects.2. A minimum aggregate of 50% in UG.
