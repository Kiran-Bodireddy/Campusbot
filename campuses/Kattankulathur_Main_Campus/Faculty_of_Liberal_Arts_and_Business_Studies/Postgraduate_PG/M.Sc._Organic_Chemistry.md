# M.Sc. Organic Chemistry

- **Degree Level**: Postgraduate (PG)
- **Awarded Credential**: M.SC
- **Faculty / College**: Faculty of Liberal Arts & Business Studies
- **Department**: Department of Chemistry
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 20
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 75,000

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
1. A pass in B.Sc. Chemistry (or) B.Sc. (Hons) Chemistry (or) B.S. Chemistry (or) B.Sc. Triple Major with Chemistry as one of the major subjects.2. A minimum aggregate of 50% in UG.
