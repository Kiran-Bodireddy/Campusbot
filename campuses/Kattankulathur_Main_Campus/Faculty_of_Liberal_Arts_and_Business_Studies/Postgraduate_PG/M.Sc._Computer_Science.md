# M.Sc. Computer Science

- **Degree Level**: Postgraduate (PG)
- **Awarded Credential**: M.Sc
- **Faculty / College**: Faculty of Liberal Arts & Business Studies
- **Department**: Department of Computer Science
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 2  Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 60
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 130,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
1. A pass in B.Sc Computer Science / BCA / B.Sc IT / B.Sc Mathematics / B.Sc Statistics/ B.Sc Physics/ B.Sc Electronics / B.Sc Triple major subjects with Computer Science as one of the Major subjects / Bachelor Degree in Computer Science Engineering or equivalent Degree from a University recognized / certified by UGC / SRMIST / AIU2. A minimum aggregate of 50% in UG.

---

## Program Overview & Curriculum Scope
Postgraduate M.Sc Computer Science programme offered by SRM Institute of Science and Technology.

## Career Opportunities
Software Development, Systems & Networks, Data & Computing, Application Engineering
