# M.Sc. Biotechnology

- **Degree Level**: Postgraduate (PG)
- **Awarded Credential**: M.Sc
- **Faculty / College**: Faculty of Liberal Arts & Business Studies
- **Department**: Department of Biotechnology – Science and Humanities
- **Campus**: Kattankulathur (KTR)
- **Duration**: 2  Years
- **Sanctioned Intake**: 100
- **Annual Tuition Fees**: INR 175,000

---

## Eligibility & Admission Criteria
1. A pass in one of B.Sc. Biotechnology (or) B.Sc. Biology (or) B.Sc. Microbiology (or) B.Sc. Biochemistry (or) B.Sc Medical Lab Technology (or)B.Sc. Agriculture (or) B.Sc. Physics (or) B.Sc. Botany (or) B.Sc. Zoology (or) B.Sc. Chemistry (or) B.Tech. Biotech (or) MBBS (or) BDS (or) BVSC (or) Genetics (or) any UG course with Biotechnology as one of the subjects.2. A minimum aggregate of 50% in UG.
